# Research figure standards

Every empirical figure is part of the research record. A visually attractive
chart without a frozen protocol and reproducible source data is not a result.

## Figure contract

Before implementing a figure, add a short contract beside its versioned plotting
command or configuration that declares:

- the scientific claim the figure may support or refute;
- the frozen experiment protocol and exact input result artifacts;
- the population, sample size, and predeclared sample-selection rule;
- the meaningful baselines and controlled ablations shown;
- the uncertainty estimator and statistical comparison, when applicable;
- the axis units, transformations, aggregation, and missing-run policy;
- the qualitative-case selection rule, if examples are displayed;
- the expected PNG and vector output paths and the caption source.

Changing the contract after observing a result requires a documented revision;
it must not silently replace an unfavorable analysis.

## Evidence requirements

- Headline comparisons use the declared three seeds when run-to-run variance is
  material and show mean with sample standard deviation or a justified confidence
  interval. Per-impression comparisons may additionally use a paired bootstrap.
- Every result panel states its sample size. Baselines and ablations share the
  same candidates, split, preprocessing, and metric implementation unless the
  difference is the explicit experimental variable.
- Qualitative panels include representative success, boundary, and failure cases.
  Their selection rule is recorded before inspecting presentation candidates;
  hand-picked favorable cases alone are prohibited.
- Architecture schematics must match the checked-in implementation and label
  planned components as planned. Figures without completed experiments are marked
  **planned** and cannot appear in a results section.

## Presentation requirements

- Use a colorblind-safe palette and do not encode meaning by color alone.
- Labels, units, legends, panel letters, error bars, and sample sizes remain
  legible at single-column paper scale. Avoid truncated axes that exaggerate
  differences; disclose any necessary truncation or nonlinear scale.
- Captions are self-contained: they identify data, protocol, metric direction,
  uncertainty, sample size, and the limited conclusion supported by the panel.
- Export empirical figures as high-resolution PNG plus PDF or SVG when the chart
  is vector-compatible. Generated files remain uncommitted unless they are an
  intentional, reviewed release artifact.

## Reproducibility and visual QA

All plotted numbers must be regenerated by a versioned command from immutable
run summaries and locked configuration. The command refuses incomplete or mixed-
protocol inputs. Before release, inspect both raster and vector outputs at paper
scale, verify the caption against the plotted data, and record the source hashes
and plotting Git revision in a compact figure manifest.
